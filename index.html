Our Manifesto

The Balcony Orchard Philosophy

Why we exist

Urban high-rises disconnected us from the soil. Most online nurseries treat plants like disposable home-decor trinkets. We built June & Jamun to return authentic, productive fruit trees to the urban homes that need them most.

Acclimatized for Containers

Open-ground farm trees won't survive in a high-rise pot. We specialize exclusively in container-acclimatized, true-to-type grafted saplings engineered to fruit early in 14-inch to 24-inch balcony pots.

Season & Climate Intelligence

Fruit gardening requires timing. We provide precise micro-climate guidance, soil science, and container architecture so your trees thrive regardless of your city's weather.

Continuous Care Support

Your journey doesn't end at delivery. Get direct post-purchase guidance, watering schedules, and disease troubleshooting from our horticulturists via WhatsApp.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about growing an orchard on your balcony.

Will a fruit tree actually grow and fruit in a balcony pot? +

Yes. Our container-acclimatized dwarf and grafted varieties (like Katimon mangoes or pink guavas) are specifically bred to maintain compact root structures while bearing full-sized fruit in 14 to 24-inch pots with adequate sunlight.

How are live plants packed so they survive shipping? +

We use rugged vertical corrugated packing that locks root moisture and protects tender branches from transit stress. You receive a pre-dispatch verification photo on WhatsApp before your tree leaves our nursery.

How do I place an order or get care advice? +

All orders and variety consultations are managed directly through our WhatsApp nursery desk to guarantee personal attention. Tap any "Adopt Sapling" button on this page to chat instantly with our team.
