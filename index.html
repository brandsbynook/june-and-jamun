Manifesto & Approach

The Balcony Orchard Story

Why we exist

Modern apartments disconnected us from the soil. Most online plant shops treat living trees like home-decor accessories—selling weak, shade-grown plants that never fruit. We built June & Jamun to return productive, heirloom fruit trees to urban homes.

For whom it's built

For urban dwellers, parents wanting children to taste real, chemical-free fruit plucked from home, and anyone seeking the quiet, grounding ritual of tending a living tree on their terrace.

How we curate

No shortcuts or forced growth. We source strictly from verified horticultural nursery belts. Every tree is a container-acclimatized graft designed to adapt to pots and bear fruit early.

The experience

Complete transparency. Clear container sizing specs, exact harvest timelines, and a dedicated WhatsApp desk to guide you long after your sapling arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about growing fruit trees on a balcony.

Will a fruit tree actually grow in a balcony pot? +

Yes. Modern container-acclimatized dwarf and grafted varieties are bred specifically to thrive and fruit in 14 to 18-inch pots with proper sunlight and organic feeding.

How are plants shipped so they arrive safely? +

We use secure vertical packaging that locks root-ball moisture and protects delicate branches. Every plant is inspected prior to dispatch.

How do I place an order or ask for advice? +

All plant selection and orders are handled directly through our WhatsApp nursery desk to ensure personalized care advice for your specific balcony sunlight.
