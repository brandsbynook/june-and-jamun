Buyer Guide & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Buying Plants Online

Everything you need to know about safe shipping and growing fruit plants in apartments.

Which fruit plants grow best in apartment balconies in India? +

Dwarf and container-acclimatized varieties perform exceptionally well with 4 to 6 hours of direct sunlight. Top recommendations include Katimon Mango, Taiwanese Pink Guava, Kagzi Lemon, Fig (Anjeer), and Pomegranate (Anar) grown in 14 to 20-inch pots.

How are live fruit plants and saplings packed for safe delivery? +

We use rugged vertical corrugated packaging that secures the root-ball moisture and protects delicate foliage from transit shock. Each plant is inspected and dispatched with pre-dispatch verification photos sent directly to your WhatsApp.

Are your fruit saplings grafted or grown from seeds? +

100% of our fruit trees are professionally grafted (true-to-type) from certified high-yielding parent varieties. This ensures your tree fruits within 1 to 2 years, unlike seed-grown plants that can take 5 to 7 years.
